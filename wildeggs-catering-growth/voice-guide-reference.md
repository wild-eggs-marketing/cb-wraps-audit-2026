# Wild Eggs Voice Reference — For Prompt Context

Pulled from the Wild Eggs Brand Messaging document. Rules are strict. Zero exceptions.

## Core position

Food brings people in. People bring them back. Wild Eggs is a scratch kitchen making breakfast and brunch from cast iron since 6:30 AM, with same-day catering. The competitive advantage is people, not product. Named staff hospitality is the actual differentiator. Copy reflects that.

## Voice rules — enforce in every draft

- **No em dashes anywhere.** Zero exceptions. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead. This is enforced universally across email, social, and web copy.
- **First-person brand voice is avoided.** Do not use "we" or "our." Use "Wild Eggs" or "the store" as the subject. This is the opposite of CBW voice.
- **No AI writing patterns.** No staccato fragment structures. No rule-of-three padding. No filler openers ("In today's fast-paced world..."). No copula constructions. No floating adjectives. No significance inflation ("game-changing," "revolutionary"). No "-ing" phrases used as filler ("driving results," "unlocking value").
- **Connected flowing prose, not punchy fragment sequences.** Sentences should link into paragraphs. Do not break every idea into its own line.
- **Lead with concrete specifics, not promotional language.** Cast-iron cinnamon roll. Housemade hollandaise. Fresh-squeezed orange juice. 6:30 AM open. Same-day catering. Real staff names where available (Kaitlyn at Landis Lakes).
- **Do not presume the occasion type.** Never say "team lunch," "meeting," or "event" as if you know what they are booking for. Leave the occasion open.
- **Do not state hours as universal claims.** Hours vary by location. Never say "Wild Eggs opens at 6:30 AM" as a blanket statement.
- **No mimosa references in online-order copy.** Dine-in context only.
- **Universal sign-off: Get cracking.** No rotating alternates. Every message ends with this.

## The switching pitch (Wild Eggs specific)

Loyalty tiers (Hatchling, Wild, Wildest) are not live yet. The switching pitch cannot use points. Lean on hospitality instead.

Ordering direct at the store's Toast link means talking to the store's actual catering coordinator. Not a third-party marketplace account manager. The person handling the last catering order remembers the account. Named coordinators (like Kaitlyn at Landis Lakes) are the differentiator. Say that.

## Anchor phrases to draw from (real WE brand copy)

- Food brings people in. People bring them back.
- Scratch kitchen making breakfast and brunch since 6:30 AM.
- Housemade hollandaise, fresh-squeezed juice, cast-iron cinnamon roll.
- Same-day catering.
- Get cracking.

**Standing rule for future edits:** any time an item's data (allergen analysis,
worker feed) contradicts its CMS description, FAQ, or tags, the fix is a
disclaimer in the user-visible copy — plus alignment of the tags/allergen
fields — in the same edit. Never leave the mismatch standing with only a note
in a report.

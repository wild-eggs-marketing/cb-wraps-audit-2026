## Executive Summary

This analysis reveals a **heavily brand-dependent keyword portfolio** with significant untapped opportunities in non-brand, local, and featured-snippet search categories.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Total Keywords Tracked (GSC):** 1,867 (across both sites)
- **Keywords in Top 10 (Positions 1-10):** ~180 keywords (9.6%)
- **Keywords in Position 8-20 (Quick Wins):** 871 keywords (46.6%) — immediate opportunity
- **Zero-Click Queries (200+ impr):** 588 keywords — featured snippet potential
- **Brand vs Non-Brand Split:** 90.4% brand / 9.6% non-brand traffic
- **Estimated Traffic Opportunity:** +2,500–3,000 clicks/month from quick wins + CTR fixes

**Critical Insight:**  
The portfolio is **over-reliant on brand searches (90.4% of clicks)**. Non-brand searches ("restaurants near me," location-specific breakfast queries) represent only 9.6% but account for **millions of impressions with near-zero CTR**. This indicates:
1. High search volume, low intent match (position 8-20)
2. Weak title/meta copy (CTR anomalies)
3. Competitor content better optimized for featured snippets (zero-click queries)

**Recommended Action Plan:**
1. **Immediate (Week 1-2):** Optimize 50 highest-impact quick-win keywords with title/meta rewrites
2. **Short-term (Month 1-2):** Target featured snippet opportunities (FAQ schema, Q&A content)
3. **Medium-term (Quarter 1):** Build location-specific content strategy for underperforming cities
4. **Long-term:** Develop non-brand discovery content to shift from 90% to 70% brand dependency

## Appendix: Glossary & Definitions

**Quick Wins:** Keywords ranking in positions 8–20 (page 2) with 200+ monthly impressions. One optimization = move to page 1.

**CTR Anomaly:** High-ranking keyword (position 1–5) with suspiciously low CTR (<2%). Indicates title/meta mismatch with search intent.

**Cannibalization:** Multiple pages ranking for the same keyword, splitting clicks and weakening both pages' authority.

**Zero-Click Query:** Query generating 200+ monthly impressions but 0–5 clicks. Usually lost to featured snippets, People Also Ask, or Google Maps results.

**Featured Snippet:** Google's highlighted answer box appearing above organic results. Wins 10–30% of clicks from the query.

**Brand Keyword:** Query containing brand name (Wild Eggs, Crazy Bowls, CBW).

**Non-Brand Keyword:** Query NOT containing brand name (breakfast near me, fried eggs, restaurants near me).

**Position:** Average ranking position in Google Search Console (1 = top, 100+ = buried).

**CTR:** Click-through rate = (clicks / impressions) × 100. Industry baseline for restaurant keywords: 0.5–2.5% depending on position & brand authority.

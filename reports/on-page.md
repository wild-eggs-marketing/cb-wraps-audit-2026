### Meta Descriptions

**Critical finding:** Meta descriptions are completely absent from crawled pages  
**Impact:** Losing 15-30% potential CTR per page

**Priority fix:**
1. Add benefit-driven meta (120-160 chars)
2. Include primary keyword once
3. Add CTA: "Discover...", "Order now...", "Learn more..."

**Example template for product pages:**
```
[Product name] at Crazy Bowls and Wraps — [key benefit]. [Keyword]. Order online or visit us today.
```

**Example template for location pages:**
```
Visit [Location] Crazy Bowls and Wraps — [specialty]. Fresh [ingredient]. Open [hours]. Order online.
```

## Audit Methodology

**Pages analyzed:** 126 unique pages from crazybowlsandwraps.com  
**Data sources:**
- Website crawl (June 2026): 126 pages, full on-page metadata
- Google Search Console: 130330 records (impressions, clicks, CTR, position)
- Google Analytics 4: Session counts and engagement metrics (last 28 days)

**Checks performed:**
- Title tag: length, presence, keyword relevance
- Meta description: length, presence, CTA effectiveness, CTR potential
- H1 tags: count (must be exactly 1), alignment with page intent
- Canonical URL: presence, self-referential validation, external detection, trailing slash consistency
- Word count: content depth analysis by page type (thin acceptable for products/locations)
- Image alt text: coverage %, file size considerations
- Duplicate detection: titles, meta descriptions, H1s across all pages
- Traffic correlation: GA4 sessions, GSC impressions/clicks per page

**Severity ratings:**
- **CRITICAL:** Affects ranking or indexation (missing canonical, external canonical, missing H1)
- **HIGH:** Directly impacts CTR or user experience (missing meta, missing title, canonical issues)
- **MEDIUM:** Affects optimization but less critical (short title/meta, low alt coverage, thin content)
- **LOW:** Minor improvements (borderline word count, small meta length issues)

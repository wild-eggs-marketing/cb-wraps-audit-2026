COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: What is schema markup?
A: JSON-LD is code that tells Google what your content means (beyond just text).
   It enables rich snippets (bigger, better results in Google), local pack
   visibility, and voice search compatibility.

Q: Why should I implement it?
A: 20-45% traffic increase within 3 months of implementation (conservative).
   It's the fastest way to improve organic search visibility.

Q: How long does it take?
A: 11-18 hours to implement all phases (spread over 4 weeks recommended).
   Ongoing: just 2-3 hours per quarter.

Q: What if I don't have all the data?
A: Most required data is available from your site. Location addresses, phone
   numbers, and hours are marked as [TO_BE_FILLED_FROM_CMS] - gather these
   from your CMS and fill in.

Q: Do I need a plugin?
A: No, but plugins make it easier. You can use Yoast ($99/year), AIOSEO
   ($99/year), or implement manually in PHP (free). See IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md
   for all 3 options.

Q: When will I see results?
A: Week 1-2: Schema deployed
   Week 3-4: Rich snippets appear in search
   Month 2-3: Traffic increase visible in analytics

Q: Is this risky?
A: No. JSON-LD doesn't change visible content, can be tested in staging first,
   can be rolled back if needed. Very low risk.

See IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md > Common Questions for more.
